Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026  ·  Effective: April 7, 2026

VITImeasure ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user. By using the VITImeasure app, you agree to this policy.

1. Who We Are

VITImeasure is an AI-powered vitiligo tracking application available on iOS and Android. Our registered contact address for privacy matters is:

VITImeasure
Email: privacy@vitimeasure.com

2. Information We Collect

2a. Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Encrypted password (we never store your plain-text password)

2b. Health & Scan Data

When you use the app to track your vitiligo, we collect:

  • Photographs of skin areas you choose to photograph
  • AI-generated analysis results (affected %, patch count, VASI score)
  • Body area location labels you provide
  • Treatment log entries (medication name, dose, dates, notes)
  • Weekly wellness check-in scores (stress, sleep, mood — rated 1–5)
  • Daily stress scores you log
Skin photographs are processed by our AI service to produce measurements. Photos are stored securely on our servers and associated only with your account. We do not sell, share, or use your photos for any purpose other than providing the tracking and analysis features of the app.

2c. Technical Data

We automatically collect limited technical information to operate the service:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version
  • API request logs (timestamps, endpoint, response codes — no request bodies)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Providing the service — to analyze your scans, store your history, and generate reports
  • Account management — to authenticate you and maintain your session
  • Cross-device sync — to keep your data consistent if you reinstall the app or use a new device
  • PDF report generation — your data is compiled locally and/or server-side only at your explicit request
  • Service improvement — aggregated, anonymised usage metrics may be used to improve accuracy and performance

We will never use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it with insurers or employers.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on servers hosted on Microsoft Azure (East US 2 region). All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher. Authentication tokens are stored in the device's secure keychain (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). Scan images are stored in secure Azure Blob Storage with access controlled by your account credentials.

While we implement industry-standard safeguards, no system is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately at privacy@vitimeasure.com.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account, all associated data — including scan images, measurements, treatment logs, and check-in history — is permanently deleted within 30 days.

Backup copies may persist for up to 90 days in disaster-recovery storage before being purged.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction — update inaccurate personal information
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Restriction — request that we restrict processing of your data
  • Objection — object to certain types of processing

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@vitimeasure.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

VITImeasure is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us so we can delete the data.

8. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate VITImeasure:

  • Microsoft Azure — cloud hosting and storage (governed by Microsoft's privacy policy)
  • Expo Push Notification Service — to deliver optional scan reminders and wellness check-in notifications

We do not embed third-party advertising SDKs or analytics platforms that track you across apps.

9. Push Notifications

If you enable scan reminders or daily stress check-ins, we use Expo's push notification service to deliver those notifications. Notification delivery tokens are associated with your account and are deleted when you disable notifications or sign out. Notifications are strictly functional — we do not send marketing messages.

10. Medical Disclaimer

VITImeasure is a personal tracking tool only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. AI-generated measurements are estimates based on image analysis and may not be accurate. Always consult a qualified dermatologist for clinical evaluation and treatment decisions.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you via an in-app notice or email at least 14 days before the change takes effect. Continued use of the app after that date constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
