2. Description of Service

VITImeasure is a personal health tracking application that uses AI image analysis to help individuals with vitiligo monitor the progression of depigmented skin areas over time. The Service includes:

  • AI-powered photograph analysis to estimate depigmented area percentages
  • VASI (Vitiligo Area Scoring Index) score computation
  • Longitudinal trend tracking and progress charts
  • Treatment log management
  • Wellness check-in logging (stress, sleep, mood)
  • PDF report generation for personal or clinical use
  • Optional reminder notifications
